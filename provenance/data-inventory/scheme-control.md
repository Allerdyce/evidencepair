| DI-12 | Personal data reporting and erasure **(was a GAP until 2026-10-01; resolved, H-06)**. Once a week, as part of the daily job, the app reports the account IDs in its audit account mapping to Atlassian's personal data reporting API. For each account Atlassian reports as closed, it erases the account ID from the audit account mapping, the history, the refusal counters, a policy's “last edited by”, and the records of changes that were previewed or in progress. Audit records stay, and verify, but no longer say who. Residual: a change previewed by that person in the seconds before the weekly check can keep their account ID until it expires. Atlassian requires every app that stores personal data to report it; the default cycle is 7 days. **Site evidence:** on the test site the daily job logged `daily-personal-data-report-done reported=2 erased=0` at 2026-10-01T22:02:01.936Z (build `f7eadfa`, the first daily with the scope) and `daily-personal-data-report-not-due` at 2026-10-05T22:02:01.653Z on the released build `ba8d3d8`.
